# Pulse Icon and Brand

## Direction
Simple enough to survive Android adaptive masking and tiny sizes.

## Recommended concept
A dark rounded background with an electric pulse/wave symbol that can also read as a stylized P. Avoid tiny details and literal lightning bolts that disappear at 24 dp.

## Android deliverables
- adaptive foreground
- adaptive background
- monochrome icon
- legacy fallback
- high-resolution marketing asset

## Motion
On launch/onboarding, a pulse line may expand into the wordmark while the app icon itself remains stable.
