===============================================================================
RESEARCH FINDINGS: iOS Feel, SF Fonts, Liquid Animations, and Liquid Glass (iOS 27)
===============================================================================

--- [STEP-BY-STEP RESEARCH OUTLINE] ---
1. Apple Human Interface Guidelines (HIG) - Core visual, spatial, and motion paradigms.
2. SF Pro Fonts - Typography scales, weights, tracking, and optical sizing.
3. Liquid Animations - Natural fluid motion, spring math, and physical-damping constants.
4. Liquid Glass (iOS 26 -> 27 WWDC 2026 Tuning) - The exact layout formulas, diffusion variables, darkened borders, and transparency settings.

--- [SECTION 1: iOS DESIGN FEEL & PARADIGMS] ---
* SPATIAL DEPTH: Elements are stacked logically on a Z-axis. Modals and overlays float over backgrounds with defined light sources, creating soft, drop-shadow falloffs.
* TEXTURE & MATTE: Materials act as physical layers (such as translucent, frosted glass) that dynamic content scrolls under.
* UNIFIED CORNER RADII: Standardized squircles (continuous curvature curves) maintain visual balance. 

--- [SECTION 2: APPLE TYPOGRAPHY (SF PRO)] ---
* SYSTEM FONTS: SF Pro Text (body text up to 19pt) and SF Pro Display (headings 20pt and above).
* DESIGN TOKENS:
  - Letter-Spacing / Tracking: Auto-scaled according to size. Displays use tighter spacing, while Text body items use wider tracking to maintain legibility.
  - Weights: Thin, Light, Regular, Medium, Semibold, Bold, Heavy, Black.
  - Optical Sizing: Dynamically changes line height and tracking properties depending on the screen resolution and font scale.

--- [SECTION 3: LIQUID MOTION & INTERPOLATION] ---
* MOTION METRICS: Transitions mimic liquid-like momentum. No linear transitions.
* SPRING EQUATIONS: High damping ratio (~0.8 to 0.9) to prevent spring oscillation while maintaining rapid initial response (high stiffness/tension, low mass).
* GESTURE CHOREOGRAPHY: Elements dynamically morph and stretch slightly during drag events, mimicking a physical fluid surface before snapping into place.

--- [SECTION 4: WWDC 2026 RETUNED LIQUID GLASS (iOS 27)] ---
With iOS 27, Apple retuned Liquid Glass to address legibility and user customization:
1. CUSTOMIZABLE TRANSPARENCY SLIDER:
   - System-wide slider introduces `--ios27-glass-transparency` (ranging from fully clear to fully tinted/opaque), replacing the old binary "Reduce Transparency" accessibility toggle.
2. STRONGER DIFFUSION METRICS:
   - Frost/blur radii are raised substantially. Busy backgrounds resolve to a calmer, diffused matte field.
3. DARKENED OUTLINING:
   - A subtle, semi-translucent dark border (`--ios27-glass-edge-color` with opacity ~0.15) surrounds glass cards to improve visual contrast against both white and black backgrounds.
4. BRIGHTER SPECULARlit RIM:
   - A high-contrast specular edge light highlight on the topmost border of the glass card conveys real lighting reflections rather than a uniform sheet.
5. LAYERED APP ICONS:
   - Custom app icons feature multi-layered glass layouts. Interactive refraction and gyroscope-derived shimmer have been refined into subtle lighting gradients.
