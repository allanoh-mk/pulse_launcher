===============================================================================
RESEARCH FINDINGS: Google Pixel Material Design (Material You / M3)
===============================================================================

--- [STEP-BY-STEP RESEARCH OUTLINE] ---
1. Material Design 3 (M3) Specification - Visual foundations, responsive shapes, and margins.
2. Material You Dynamic Color - The Monet engine, HCT color space, and color palettes.
3. System Typography - Google Sans, Roboto, and Jetpack Compose text styles.
4. Pixel Launcher Transitions - Motion behaviors, system gestures, and layout corners.

--- [SECTION 1: MATERIAL DESIGN 3 (M3) PRINCIPLES] ---
* RADICAL ROUNDNESS: Corners use large, expressive radii. Cards typically use 12dp, 16dp, or 28dp, and buttons are shaped as capsules or rounded rectangles.
* ELEVATION BY COLOR: Rather than using deep, physical dropshadows, M3 relies on tonal color overlays to convey stack height and layout hierarchies.
* GRID SPACING: Formulated around an 8dp grid system, with 16dp and 24dp margins for container padding.

--- [SECTION 2: DYNAMIC COLOR & MONET ENGINE] ---
* HCT COLOR SPACE: Material You utilizes Hue, Chroma, and Tone (HCT) rather than RGB or HSL, maintaining consistent visual contrast across dark and light modes.
* PALETTE EXTRACTION:
  - Wallpaper extraction parses colors using Palette KTX (`androidx.palette:palette-ktx`).
  - Core engine selects a primary seed color based on prominence and colorfulness.
  - Dynamically constructs five tonal palettes: Accent 1, Accent 2, Accent 3, Neutral 1, Neutral 2.
  - Tonal steps (e.g. Tone 10, Tone 20... Tone 90) determine background and outline mappings.

--- [SECTION 3: PIXEL MOTION & SPRING TRANSLATIONS] ---
* PIXEL ANIMATIONS: UI scaling uses overshoot and dampening springs, ensuring that dragging an icon or expanding a panel feels connected to the finger.
* COLLAPSIBLE SEARCH BAR (QSB): Quick Search Bar morphs seamlessly from a pill in the dock into a full-screen search view using shared element bounds.
