--- [SECTION 1: SPRING PHYSICS MATHEMATICS] ---
Unlike linear or bezier easing curves, spring physics calculate forces dynamically based on physical coefficients:
* HOOKE'S LAW: $F = -kx - cv$
  - $k$ represents **stiffness** (force constant).
  - $c$ represents **damping** (velocity reduction).
  - $x$ represents **displacement** (distance from target).
* DAMPING RATIO ($\zeta$):
  - $\zeta = 1.0$: **Critically Damped** (Snaps to target as quickly as possible without overshooting).
  - $\zeta < 1.0$: **Underdamped** (Overshoots and oscillates slightly before settling; great for bouncy, organic transitions).
  - $\zeta > 1.0$: **Overdamped** (Slow, sluggish approach without overshooting).
* FOR LAUNCHERS: Typically standard on underdamped values ($\zeta \approx 0.7$ to 0.85) to create responsive, tactile bounceback, paired with medium stiffness ($k \approx 300$ to 500) for instant feedback.

--- [SECTION 2: FRAME CYCLE SYNCHRONIZATION] ---
* ANDROID CHOREOGRAPHER: Coordinates system drawing cycles. Syncs layout animations exactly with the hardware display's VSYNC pulses (60Hz, 90Hz, or 120Hz LTPO panels).
* REFRESH RATE ADAPTATION: To avoid jank, calculations use delta time ($\Delta t$) instead of assuming constant frame ticks, maintaining consistent physics speeds even during sudden frame-rate drops.

--- [SECTION 3: COMPOSE PHYSICS ARSENAL] ---
* `Animatable`: Jetpack Compose's state-driven animation wrapper. Supports launching spring animations via `animateTo(targetValue, spring(stiffness, dampingRatio))`.
* `sh.calvin.reorderable`: Uses smooth spring-based displacement curves during list drag-and-drop operations, ensuring elements slide out of the way before snapping to their sorted positions.
